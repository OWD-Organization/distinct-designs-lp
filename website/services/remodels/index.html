Remodels

Kitchen, bathroom, and addition remodels

Designer kitchen renovations and remodels, and luxury bathroom renovations, remodels and additions. A whole-home remodel is a separate service.

A smaller scope than a whole-home remodel

The homepage FAQ lists these on their own, next to whole-home renovations: Designer Kitchen Renovations and Remodels, and Luxury Bathroom Renovations, Remodels and Additions.

The services page describes remodeling as a complete structural transformation or a targeted renovation of the spaces that matter most. A whole-home remodel prices the kitchen, the baths, and the rest of the house as one project. That service is Whole House Remodel.

We specialize in custom luxury kitchen and bathroom remodels, custom tile and stone, backsplashes, showers, mosaics, and floors.

Kitchens

  • Luxury kitchen redesigns and custom layouts
  • Custom cabinetry and built-in storage
  • Designer backsplashes and feature tile

Bathrooms

  • Spa-style bathrooms and steam rooms
  • Designer shower systems and soaking tubs
  • Custom vanities and bespoke storage

Additions are named in that same FAQ line, with the bathroom renovations and remodels. No separate scope for an addition is published.
